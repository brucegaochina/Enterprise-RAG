# Parental and Family Leave Policy

Document ID: HR-LV-012. Owner: People Operations. Effective January 1, 2026.

## Eligibility

All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for paid parental leave after 90 days of employment. Leave must be completed within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

## Paid Parental Leave

| Situation | Paid leave |
| Birthing parent | 16 weeks at 100% of base pay |
| Non-birthing parent | 12 weeks at 100% of base pay |
| Adoptive or foster parent | 12 weeks at 100% of base pay |

Parental leave can be taken all at once or in up to three separate blocks of at least two weeks each. Helios pays the difference between any government or state disability benefits and 100% of base pay. Parental leave runs at the same time as leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) where applicable.

## Returning to Work

After parental leave, employees can use a 4-week phased return, working a 60% schedule while receiving full pay. Nursing parents have access to private lactation rooms in every Helios office, and Helios reimburses the cost of shipping breast milk during business travel.

## Requesting Leave

Notify your manager and submit a leave request in Workday at least 30 days before the expected start of leave, or as soon as possible for unexpected situations. The Leave Team, reachable at leave@heliosdynamics.example, will contact you within 2 business days to explain the next steps.

## Family Caregiver Leave

Employees can take up to 4 weeks of paid caregiver leave per year to care for a spouse, partner, child, or parent with a serious health condition. Caregiver leave can be taken in increments as small as one day.

## Family Building Benefits

Helios provides a lifetime family-building benefit of up to $20,000 per employee through the Bloom Family program. This benefit can be used for fertility treatment, egg freezing, surrogacy, and adoption expenses.

## Job Protection

Employees on approved parental or caregiver leave return to the same or an equivalent position with the same pay and benefits. Health benefits continue during leave on the same terms as for active employees.
