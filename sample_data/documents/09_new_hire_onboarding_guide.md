# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Document ID: HR-ONB-009. Owner: People Operations, Onboarding Team.

## Before Your First Day

About one week before your start date, you will receive a welcome email from onboarding@heliosdynamics.example with your Okta activation link. Your laptop is shipped to your home address 5 business days before your start date for remote and hybrid employees. Office-based employees pick up their laptop at the IT desk on day one. Complete your I-9 or equivalent work authorization documents in Workday before 9:00 a.m. on your first day.

## Day One

| Time | Activity |
| 9:00 a.m. | Welcome session with People Operations (virtual) |
| 10:30 a.m. | IT setup: Okta, MFA, VPN, Slack, and email |
| 12:00 p.m. | Team lunch, paid by Helios |
| 1:30 p.m. | Meeting with your manager to review your 30-60-90 day plan |
| 3:00 p.m. | Meet your onboarding buddy |

## Onboarding Buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first 90 days. Your buddy helps you understand how things work, introduces you to colleagues, and answers questions you might not want to ask your manager. Buddies meet with new hires at least once a week during the first month.

## Required Training

The following training must be completed in the Helios Academy learning platform within 30 days of your start date:

- Security awareness training (about 45 minutes)
- Code of Conduct and annual certification (about 30 minutes)
- Harassment prevention training (about 60 minutes; managers take a 2-hour version)
- Data privacy fundamentals (about 30 minutes)

Engineers must also complete Secure Coding 101 within 60 days.

## Benefits Enrollment

You have 30 days from your start date to enroll in benefits in Workday. If you miss the deadline, you are enrolled in Helios Standard with employee-only coverage and can only change plans during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event. The 401(k) plan automatically enrolls you at a 4% contribution rate, which you can change at any time.

## The 30-60-90 Day Plan

- First 30 days: learn the products, the customers, and your team's goals. Complete all required training.
- Days 31 to 60: take ownership of a first project and deliver an early win.
- Days 61 to 90: work independently and share feedback about onboarding in the 90-day survey.

Your manager holds a formal check-in at day 30 and day 90.
