# Procurement and Vendor Management Policy

Document ID: FIN-PROC-015. Policy owner: Procurement, Finance. Effective January 2026.

## Purpose

This policy makes sure that Helios Dynamics buys goods and services at the best value, from trustworthy vendors, with the right approvals.

## Purchasing Thresholds

| Purchase amount | Process |
| Under $5,000 | Manager approval; pay with corporate card or a Coupa requisition |
| $5,000 to $50,000 | Purchase order in Coupa and at least 2 competitive quotes |
| Above $50,000 | Formal request for proposal (RFP) with at least 3 bids, plus Finance and Legal review |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

## New Vendor Onboarding

New vendors are onboarded in Coupa. Every new vendor must provide a W-9 or equivalent tax form, banking details, and proof of insurance. Vendors that will access, store, or process Confidential or Restricted data must complete the Vendor Security Questionnaire and pass a security review before a contract is signed. Security reviews take about 10 business days.

## Preferred Vendors

Procurement maintains a list of preferred vendors with negotiated pricing, published in Coupa. Use a preferred vendor whenever one is available. Examples include Dell and Apple for hardware, AWS for cloud infrastructure, and Staples for office supplies.

## Contracts and Signature Authority

Only authorized signatories can sign contracts on behalf of Helios. Directors can sign contracts up to $50,000 per year. Vice Presidents can sign contracts up to $250,000 per year. Contracts above $250,000 must be signed by the Chief Financial Officer. All contracts must be reviewed by Legal before signature, except for purchases on standard Helios terms under $25,000.

## Payment Terms

The standard payment term for vendors is Net 45. Shorter terms require approval from the Controller. Invoices must reference a valid purchase order number, or they are returned to the vendor.

## Vendor Performance

Business owners must review strategic vendors every year, considering quality, delivery, price, and security. Underperforming vendors are placed on an improvement plan or replaced.

## Ethics in Procurement

Employees involved in vendor selection must disclose any conflicts of interest using form COI-1 and must follow the gift limits in the Code of Business Conduct.
