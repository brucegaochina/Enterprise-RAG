# Helios Product Catalog 2026

For internal sales and support use. Prices are US list prices in USD, effective January 2026.

## Product Overview

Helios Dynamics sells three families of products: wireless sensors that attach to industrial equipment, edge gateways that collect sensor data on the factory floor, and the Helios Cloud Platform, which analyzes data and predicts equipment failures.

## HX-310 Vibration Sensor

The HX-310 is a wireless tri-axial vibration and temperature sensor for motors, pumps, fans, and gearboxes. It samples vibration up to 25.6 kHz and uses on-device machine learning to detect bearing wear and misalignment.

- Battery life: up to 5 years at one reading per hour
- Ingress protection: IP68
- Operating temperature: -40 to 85 degrees Celsius
- Certifications: ATEX and IECEx Zone 1 for hazardous environments
- List price: $289 per sensor

## HX-450 Thermal Camera

The HX-450 is a fixed thermal imaging camera for electrical cabinets, conveyors, and kilns. It detects hot spots with an accuracy of plus or minus 2 degrees Celsius.

- Resolution: 320 x 240 thermal pixels
- Ingress protection: IP67
- Power: Power over Ethernet (PoE)
- List price: $1,950 per camera

## HX-220 Edge Gateway

The HX-220 Edge Gateway connects up to 200 Helios sensors and forwards data to the Helios Cloud Platform. It can run analytics locally when the internet connection is lost and stores up to 30 days of data offline.

- Connectivity: Ethernet, Wi-Fi 6, and 5G cellular
- Protocols: OPC UA, Modbus TCP, and MQTT
- Operating temperature: -20 to 60 degrees Celsius
- List price: $1,200 per gateway
