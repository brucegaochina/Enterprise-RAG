# Customer Support Service Level Agreement

Document ID: CS-SLA-018. Applies to all Helios Cloud Platform customers. Version 2026.1.

## Support Tiers

| Support tier | Included with | Support hours | Channels |
| Standard | Starter plan | Business hours, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. customer local time | Email and support portal |
| Premium | Pro plan | 24/5 | Email, portal, and phone |
| Enterprise | Enterprise plan | 24/7/365 | Email, portal, phone, and a dedicated Slack channel |

## Response Time Targets

| Priority | Definition | Standard | Premium | Enterprise |
| P1 Critical | Platform unavailable or data loss | 4 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| P2 High | Major feature impaired, no workaround | 8 hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P3 Normal | Minor issue with a workaround | 2 business days | 1 business day | 4 hours |
| P4 Low | Questions and feature requests | 5 business days | 3 business days | 1 business day |

Response time is the time between ticket creation and the first response from a qualified support engineer.

## Uptime Commitment

Helios commits to a monthly uptime of 99.9% for the Pro plan and 99.95% for the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan has no uptime commitment. Scheduled maintenance, announced at least 5 days in advance and limited to 4 hours per month, is excluded from the uptime calculation.

## Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers can request service credits.

| Monthly uptime | Service credit (percent of monthly fee) |
| Below the commitment but at least 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 99.0% but at least 95.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month and are applied to the next invoice.

## Escalation Path

If a customer is not satisfied with the progress of a ticket, they can escalate to the Support Duty Manager. P1 incidents are escalated automatically to the Director of Customer Support after 2 hours and to the VP of Customer Success after 4 hours.

## Hardware Replacement (RMA)

Defective hardware under warranty is replaced through a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA). Standard and Premium customers ship the defective device back, and a replacement is sent within 5 business days of receipt. Enterprise customers receive advance replacement: the new device ships the next business day, before the defective device is returned.
