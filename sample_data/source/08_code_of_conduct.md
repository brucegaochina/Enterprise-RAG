# Code of Business Conduct and Ethics

Document ID: LEG-COC-008. Approved by the Board of Directors, January 2026.

## Our Commitment

Every Helios Dynamics employee, officer, and director must follow this Code. Managers have an extra responsibility to lead by example and to create an environment where people feel safe raising concerns.

## Respectful Workplace

Helios prohibits harassment, discrimination, and bullying of any kind. This includes unwelcome comments, jokes, or conduct related to any protected characteristic, as well as sexual harassment. The policy applies in offices, at customer sites, at company events, and online.

## Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest exists when personal interests could influence, or appear to influence, your business decisions. Examples include owning a significant stake in a supplier, hiring a family member, or holding a second job with a competitor. Employees must disclose potential conflicts using form COI-1 in the People Hub when they arise and confirm their disclosures every year during the annual Code certification in February.

## Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may accept gifts of modest value, up to a total of $100 per vendor per calendar year. Cash and cash equivalents such as gift cards are never acceptable. Meals and entertainment with customers or vendors are allowed if they are reasonable, infrequent, and have a clear business purpose. Gifts to government officials require prior approval from Legal.

## Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Helios prohibits bribery in every form. Employees and partners must never offer, promise, or give anything of value to improperly influence a decision. This applies to public officials and private companies, and complies with the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the UK Bribery Act. Facilitation payments are prohibited.

## Protecting Company Assets and Information

Company assets, including equipment, intellectual property, and confidential information, must be used only for legitimate business purposes. Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends.

## Fair Competition

Employees must never discuss prices, bids, or market allocation with competitors. Competitive information must be obtained only from legitimate public sources.

## Raising Concerns

Report suspected violations to your manager, HR, Legal, or the Ethics Hotline. The Ethics Hotline is operated by EthicsPoint, an independent provider, and is available 24/7 at 1-888-555-0199 or online. Reports can be made anonymously where local law allows.

## Non-Retaliation

Helios strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of this Code.

## Consequences

Violations of the Code can result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment, and may also be reported to authorities.
