# 401(k) Retirement Savings Plan Summary

Document ID: HR-RET-011. Plan year 2026. Plan administrator: Summit Retirement Services.

## Eligibility

All US employees aged 18 or older are eligible to participate in the Helios Dynamics 401(k) plan from their first day of employment, including part-time employees.

## Automatic Enrollment

New employees are automatically enrolled at a pre-tax contribution rate of 4% of eligible pay. Contributions automatically increase by 1% every April 1 until they reach 10%. Employees can change their contribution rate, switch to Roth contributions, or opt out at any time on the Summit Retirement website.

## Company Match

Helios matches 100% of the first 4% of pay that you contribute, plus 50% of the next 2%. If you contribute 6% or more, you receive the maximum company match of 5% of eligible pay. The match is calculated and deposited every pay period.

## Vesting

Your own contributions and their investment earnings are always 100% vested. Company matching contributions vest according to a 3-year graded schedule.

| Years of service | Vested percentage of company match |
| Less than 1 year | 0% |
| 1 year | 33% |
| 2 years | 67% |
| 3 years or more | 100% |

## 2026 Contribution Limits

The IRS limit for employee contributions in 2026 is $24,500. Employees aged 50 or older can make additional catch-up contributions of up to $8,000.

## Investment Options

The default investment is a Summit Target Date Fund based on your expected retirement year. Participants can choose from 24 funds, including index funds with expense ratios as low as 0.03%. A self-directed brokerage window is also available.

## Loans and Withdrawals

Participants can borrow up to 50% of their vested balance, with a maximum of $50,000. Only one loan can be outstanding at a time, and loans are repaid through payroll deductions over up to 5 years. Hardship withdrawals are allowed for IRS-approved reasons, such as preventing eviction or paying medical bills.
