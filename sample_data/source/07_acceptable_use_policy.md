# Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: HD-SEC-002. Policy owner: IT and Security. Last reviewed March 2026.

## Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of Helios Dynamics devices, networks, software, and online services. It complements the Information Security Policy (HD-SEC-001).

## Personal Use

Limited personal use of company devices is allowed, as long as it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or violate any law or Helios policy. Employees should have no expectation of privacy on company devices and networks. Helios monitors network traffic and endpoint activity for security purposes.

## Prohibited Activities

- Cryptocurrency mining on any company device or cloud account.
- Peer-to-peer file sharing and torrent clients.
- Installing software that is not approved in the ServiceNow software catalog.
- Disabling or bypassing endpoint protection, VPN, or device management.
- Accessing, storing, or sending offensive, harassing, or illegal content.
- Using company email to run a personal business.

## Software Requests

Software that is not in the self-service catalog can be requested in ServiceNow using the Software Request form. Security reviews new software within 5 business days. Browser extensions must also be approved, because they can read sensitive data.

## Generative AI Tools

Employees may use the following approved generative AI tools for work: Helios Copilot, the internal assistant, and ChatGPT Enterprise through the company workspace. GitHub Copilot is approved for engineering teams.

- Never paste Confidential or Restricted data into AI tools that are not approved.
- Always review AI-generated content for accuracy before sharing it with customers.
- AI-generated code must go through normal code review.
- Do not use AI tools to make decisions about hiring, promotion, or termination.

## Email and Messaging

Use your company email and Slack for business communication only. Do not forward company email to personal accounts. External Slack Connect channels require approval from IT. Messages in Slack are retained for 18 months, as described in the Data Retention and Privacy Policy.

## Social Media

Only authorized spokespeople may speak on behalf of Helios Dynamics. When posting about the industry, employees should make clear that the views are their own. Never share unreleased product information, customer names, or financial results before they are public.

## Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in loss of access, disciplinary action, or termination, and may be reported to law enforcement when required.
