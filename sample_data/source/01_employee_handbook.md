# Helios Dynamics Employee Handbook

Version 7.2, revised February 2026. Policy owner: People Operations.

## Welcome to Helios Dynamics

Helios Dynamics designs industrial IoT sensors, edge gateways, and the Helios Cloud Platform that manufacturers use to monitor equipment health. The company was founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas, and today employs approximately 2,400 people across offices in Austin, Denver, Toronto, Dublin, and Singapore. Our chief executive officer is Maya Lindqvist.

Our mission is to help every factory run safer, cleaner, and with zero unplanned downtime.

## Our Values

- Customers first: we measure success by the uptime of our customers.
- Own the outcome: we take responsibility end to end.
- Build with integrity: we do the right thing even when nobody is watching.
- Learn every day: we share knowledge generously.

## Working Hours

The standard work week is 40 hours. Helios uses core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in your local time zone, during which employees are expected to be available for meetings. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedule with their manager.

Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked in Workday. Overtime is paid at one and a half times the regular rate for hours worked above 40 in a week and must be approved in advance by the employee's manager.

## Pay Schedule

Employees are paid biweekly on Fridays, for a total of 26 pay periods per year. Pay statements are available in Workday under Pay. If a payday falls on a bank holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day. Report payroll errors to payroll@heliosdynamics.example within 10 business days.

## Equal Employment Opportunity

Helios Dynamics is an equal opportunity employer. Employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications, and business needs, and never on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

## Dress Code

Helios has a business casual dress code in offices. Employees visiting customer manufacturing sites must wear the personal protective equipment required by that site, including safety glasses and steel-toe shoes. Helios reimburses up to $200 per year for safety footwear.

## Office Access

Every employee receives a photo badge on their first day. Badges must be visible at all times in Helios offices. Lost badges must be reported to facilities@heliosdynamics.example the same day. Visitors must be registered in the Envoy system at least 24 hours in advance and escorted at all times.

## Open Door Policy

Employees are encouraged to raise questions or concerns with their manager, their HR Business Partner, or any member of the leadership team. Concerns can also be reported anonymously through the Ethics Hotline described in the Code of Conduct.
