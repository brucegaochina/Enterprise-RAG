# Performance Review Process

Document ID: HR-PERF-010. Owner: People Operations. Applies to the 2026 performance year.

## Review Cycles

Helios Dynamics runs two review cycles each year. The mid-year review takes place in June and focuses on feedback and development; it does not include a rating. The annual review takes place in December and includes a performance rating, which determines merit increases and bonuses.

Employees who joined after October 1 are not rated in the annual review for that year.

## Steps of the Annual Review

1. Self-assessment: employees reflect on their goals and accomplishments in Workday (due December 5).
2. Peer feedback: each employee requests feedback from 3 to 5 colleagues (due December 10).
3. Manager assessment: the manager writes the review and proposes a rating (due December 15).
4. Calibration: managers in each department meet to compare ratings and ensure consistency.
5. Conversation: managers share the final review with each employee in January.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Description |
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently far exceeds expectations; a role model |
| 4 | Exceeds | Frequently exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Achieves | Fully meets expectations; solid performance |
| 2 | Partially achieves | Meets some expectations; improvement needed |
| 1 | Does not achieve | Does not meet expectations |

## Merit Increases and Bonuses

Merit increases are effective March 1 and appear in the first paycheck of March. The annual bonus is paid in March and is based on company performance and the individual rating.

| Job level | Target bonus (percent of base salary) |
| Individual contributor levels 1 to 3 | 8% |
| Individual contributor levels 4 to 5 | 12% |
| Manager | 15% |
| Director | 20% |
| Vice President | 30% |

An Exceptional rating can result in up to 150% of target bonus; an Achieves rating typically results in 100% of target.

## Promotions

Promotion nominations are reviewed twice a year, in March and September. Managers submit a promotion case describing how the employee already performs at the next level. Promotions take effect on April 1 or October 1.

## Performance Improvement Plans

An employee rated Partially achieves or Does not achieve may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP lasts 60 days and includes clear, measurable goals and weekly check-ins with the manager. HR must approve every PIP before it begins.
