# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Document ID: HR-RW-005. Policy owner: People Operations. Effective April 1, 2026.

## Work Arrangements

Every role at Helios Dynamics is assigned one of three work arrangements, which is recorded in Workday.

- Office-based: the employee works from a Helios office five days a week. This applies mainly to lab, manufacturing, and facilities roles.
- Hybrid: the employee works from their assigned office three days a week. Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are anchor days when hybrid teams are expected in the office.
- Remote: the employee works from home and is not assigned to an office. Remote employees are expected to attend in-person team gatherings up to four times per year, with travel paid by Helios.

Changes to a work arrangement require approval from the employee's manager and their HR Business Partner.

## Home Office Setup

Hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $1,000 for a desk, chair, and accessories, plus a monthly internet allowance of $50 paid through payroll. IT provides a laptop, a 27-inch monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, and a headset. Employees can request a free virtual ergonomic assessment through the People Hub.

## Core Hours and Availability

All employees should be available during core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in their local time zone. Teams spread across time zones should agree on at least 3 hours of overlap per day. Status in Slack must reflect when you are away for more than one hour.

## Working From Another Location

Employees may work from a location other than their usual home for up to 4 weeks per calendar year in countries where Helios has a legal entity: the United States, Canada, Ireland, and Singapore. Working from any other country requires prior approval from People Operations and Legal because of tax and immigration rules.

Working from a different US state or Canadian province for more than 30 consecutive days requires HR approval, because it can change payroll tax obligations.

## Security Requirements for Remote Work

- Always connect to company systems through the GlobalProtect VPN when working outside a Helios office.
- Never use public computers to access company data.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.
- Do not let family members or friends use company devices.
- Printing Confidential or Restricted documents at home is not allowed.

## Coworking Spaces

Remote employees who live more than 50 miles from a Helios office can be reimbursed up to $300 per month for a coworking membership, with manager approval.
