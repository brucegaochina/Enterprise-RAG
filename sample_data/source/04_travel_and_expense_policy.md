# Travel and Expense Policy

Document ID: FIN-TE-004. Policy owner: Finance. Effective March 1, 2026.

## Purpose

This policy explains how employees book business travel and get reimbursed for business expenses. Employees should spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own.

## Booking Travel

All flights, hotels, and rental cars must be booked through TripLink, the company travel platform. Bookings made outside TripLink are reimbursed only if they cost less than the lowest TripLink fare and are approved by your manager. Trips must be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever possible.

## Air Travel

- Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
- Business class is allowed for flights longer than 10 hours with written approval from a Vice President.
- Seat selection fees up to $50 per flight and one checked bag are reimbursable.

## Hotel Limits

Hotel nightly rates, excluding taxes, must not exceed the limits below.

| City category | Nightly limit |
| Standard cities | $200 |
| High-cost cities: New York, San Francisco, Boston, London, Zurich, Singapore | $325 |
| Helios office cities: Austin, Denver, Toronto, Dublin | $225 |

If no hotel is available within the limit, document the exception in TripLink and get manager approval before booking.

## Meals

The daily meal per diem is $75 for domestic travel and $90 for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except for approved client entertainment with a maximum of $150 per person including food. Meals provided by a conference or customer are deducted from the per diem.

## Ground Transportation

Use economy rideshare, taxis, or public transit. Rental cars must be compact or midsize unless four or more employees travel together. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile. Parking at the airport is reimbursed up to $25 per day.

## International Travel

Employees traveling internationally must register their trip with International SOS at least 7 days before departure. Laptops taken to high-risk countries must be loaner devices issued by IT. Visa fees and required vaccinations are reimbursable.

## Submitting Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the expense. Itemized receipts are required for every expense over $25. Expenses submitted more than 90 days late will not be reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll after approval.

## Approval Limits

| Expense report total | Required approver |
| Up to $5,000 | Direct manager |
| $5,000 to $25,000 | Director |
| Above $25,000 | Vice President and Finance |

## Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than four times a year receive an American Express corporate card. The card must never be used for personal purchases. Personal charges must be repaid within 15 days and may result in the card being cancelled.
