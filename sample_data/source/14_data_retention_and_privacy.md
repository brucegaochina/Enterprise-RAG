# Data Retention and Privacy Policy

Document ID: HD-PRIV-002. Policy owner: Data Protection Officer. Approved February 2026.

## Purpose

This policy defines how long Helios Dynamics keeps records and how personal data is protected, in compliance with the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), Canada's PIPEDA, and Singapore's PDPA.

## Retention Schedule

| Record type | Retention period |
| Financial and tax records | 7 years after the end of the fiscal year |
| Employee personnel files | 7 years after termination |
| Payroll records | 7 years |
| Customer contracts | 10 years after contract expiry |
| Customer telemetry data | 24 months, unless the customer contract states otherwise |
| Email | 3 years |
| Slack messages | 18 months |
| Security and audit logs | 1 year online, 3 years in archive |
| Job applicant records | 2 years after the hiring decision |
| Support tickets | 5 years |

Records must be deleted or anonymized at the end of their retention period, unless a legal hold applies.

## Legal Holds

When litigation, an audit, or a government investigation is expected, Legal may issue a legal hold. A legal hold overrides the retention schedule. Employees who receive a legal hold notice must not delete, change, or move the affected records until Legal releases the hold.

## Personal Data Principles

- Collect only the personal data needed for a specific purpose.
- Use personal data only for the purpose for which it was collected.
- Keep personal data accurate and up to date.
- Protect personal data with appropriate security measures, as defined in HD-SEC-001.

## Data Subject Requests

Individuals can ask to access, correct, delete, or export their personal data. All data subject access requests (DSARs) must be forwarded to privacy@heliosdynamics.example within 2 business days of receipt. Helios must respond within 30 days, or within 45 days for requests under the CCPA.

## International Data Transfers

Personal data transferred from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom to other countries is protected by Standard Contractual Clauses. Customer telemetry data for EU customers is stored in the Helios Cloud region in Frankfurt.

## Personal Data Breaches

Suspected personal data breaches must be reported to the Security team immediately. When a breach is confirmed, Helios notifies the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours, as required by the GDPR.
