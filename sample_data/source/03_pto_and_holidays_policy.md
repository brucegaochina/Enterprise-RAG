# PTO and Holidays Policy

Document ID: HR-PTO-003. Applies to all full-time employees in the United States and Canada. Last updated January 2026.

## Paid Time Off Allowance

Paid time off (PTO) accrues each pay period based on completed years of service.

| Completed years of service | Annual PTO days | Accrual per pay period |
| 0 to 2 years | 15 days | 4.62 hours |
| 3 to 5 years | 20 days | 6.15 hours |
| 6 or more years | 25 days | 7.69 hours |

New employees can use PTO as soon as it has accrued. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover

Employees can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire. Unused days above the carryover limit are forfeited on December 31, except where state law requires otherwise.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit PTO requests in Workday. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Managers should respond within 3 business days. During the last two weeks of each fiscal quarter, the Finance and Sales teams may restrict PTO requests.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Every employee receives 10 paid sick days per year, available from the first day of employment. Sick days can be used for your own illness, medical appointments, or to care for a family member. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out at termination. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than 5 consecutive working days.

## Company Holidays

Helios observes 11 paid company holidays in the United States: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day.

In addition, every employee receives 2 floating holidays per year that can be used for any personal, cultural, or religious observance. Floating holidays do not carry over.

## Winter Shutdown

Offices close from December 24 through January 1. Days of the winter shutdown that are not company holidays are granted as paid shutdown days and do not count against PTO. Employees in on-call rotations receive equivalent time off in January.

## Other Paid Leave

- Bereavement leave: 5 paid days for the loss of an immediate family member and 3 paid days for an extended family member.
- Jury duty: full pay for up to 10 days of jury service per year.
- Volunteer time off: 2 paid days (16 hours) per year for volunteering with registered nonprofit organizations.
- Voting: up to 2 paid hours to vote in public elections.

## PTO Payout at Termination

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out at termination where required by state or provincial law, including Colorado and California. In other locations, accrued PTO up to a maximum of 10 days is paid out at termination.
