# Q3 2026 Quarterly Business Review

Confidential. Prepared by Finance and Strategy for the executive leadership team. Quarter ended September 30, 2026.

## Executive Summary

Helios Dynamics delivered record revenue of $148.6 million in Q3 2026, up 22% year over year, driven by strong growth in cloud subscriptions and two large automotive deals in EMEA. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $305 million. Gross margin improved to 58.4%, compared with 55.1% in Q3 2025.

## Revenue by Region

| Region | Q3 2026 revenue | Year-over-year growth |
| North America | $82.4 million | 18% |
| EMEA | $41.3 million | 31% |
| APAC | $24.9 million | 24% |
| Total | $148.6 million | 22% |

## Revenue by Segment

| Segment | Q3 2026 revenue | Share of total |
| Cloud subscriptions | $71.9 million | 48% |
| Hardware | $61.2 million | 41% |
| Professional services | $15.5 million | 11% |

## Key Metrics

- Total customers: 1,940, up from 1,610 a year ago.
- Net revenue retention: 118%.
- Gross revenue retention: 94%.
- Sensors deployed: 1.3 million, with 210,000 added in the quarter.
- Headcount at quarter end: 2,412 employees.

## Highlights

- Signed the largest contract in company history: a 3-year, $18 million Enterprise agreement with a European automotive manufacturer covering 14 plants.
- Launched the HX-450 Thermal Camera, which generated $3.8 million in its first quarter.
- Opened the Singapore office expansion, adding capacity for 150 employees.
- Helios Cloud achieved 99.98% uptime during the quarter.

## Challenges and Risks

- Component lead times for 5G modules increased to 26 weeks, delaying some HX-220 gateway shipments.
- Average sales cycle for Enterprise deals grew from 5.5 to 6.8 months.
- Currency headwinds reduced EMEA revenue by approximately $1.1 million.

## Q4 2026 Outlook

Finance expects Q4 2026 revenue between $155 million and $160 million and full-year 2026 revenue between $565 million and $570 million. The company plans to add 120 employees in Q4, mainly in engineering and customer success.
