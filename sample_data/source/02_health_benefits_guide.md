# Helios Dynamics 2026 Health Benefits Guide

Effective January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026. Policy owner: People Operations, Benefits Team. Document ID: HR-BEN-2026.

## Overview

Helios Dynamics offers two medical plans to all full-time employees who work 30 or more hours per week: Helios Standard and Helios Plus. Both plans are administered by BlueRiver Health and use the BlueRiver Select provider network. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date. Open enrollment runs from November 3 to November 21 each year, and elections take effect on January 1.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Helios Standard | Helios Plus |
| Employee-only monthly premium | $45 | $120 |
| Family monthly premium | $310 | $520 |
| Annual deductible (individual) | $1,500 | $500 |
| Out-of-pocket maximum (individual) | $5,000 | $2,500 |
| Primary care visit | $25 copay | $10 copay |
| Specialist visit | $50 copay | $30 copay |
| Emergency room | $250 after deductible | $150 copay |
| HSA eligible | Yes | No |

Helios Standard is a high-deductible plan that is eligible for a Health Savings Account (HSA). Helios contributes $750 per year for individual coverage and $1,500 per year for family coverage to the HSA of every employee enrolled in Helios Standard.

## Vision Care

Both Helios Standard and Helios Plus include vision coverage. Annual eye exams are covered at 100% with an in-network optometrist, once every 12 months, under both plans.

Helios Standard covers one pair of glasses every 24 months, with a frame allowance of up to $150. Contact lenses are not covered under Helios Standard.

Helios Plus covers glasses or contact lenses every 12 months, with a $200 allowance for frames or a $180 allowance for contact lenses. LASIK surgery is not covered by Helios Standard. Helios Plus members receive a 15% discount on LASIK through the VisionFirst network.

## Hearing Care

Annual hearing exams are covered at 100% under both plans when performed by an in-network audiologist.

Hearing aids are covered under both plans. Helios Standard pays up to $1,000 per ear every 36 months. Helios Plus pays up to $2,500 per ear every 36 months, which includes the fitting appointment and one year of batteries. Cochlear implants and bone-anchored hearing systems are covered under the medical benefit after the deductible on both plans when they are medically necessary.

Out-of-network hearing providers are not covered under Helios Standard. Helios Plus reimburses out-of-network hearing services at 60% after the deductible.

## Dental Care

Dental coverage is a separate plan called Helios Dental. It costs $12 per month for employee-only coverage and $38 per month for family coverage. Preventive care, including two cleanings per year and annual x-rays, is covered at 100%. Basic services such as fillings are covered at 80%, and major services such as crowns and bridges are covered at 50%. The annual maximum benefit is $2,000 per person. Orthodontia is available for dependents under age 19 with a lifetime maximum of $1,500.

## Mental Health

Outpatient therapy is covered by both medical plans: a $25 copay per session under Helios Standard and a $10 copay per session under Helios Plus. In addition, every employee and household member can use the MindBridge Employee Assistance Program (EAP), which provides 8 free, confidential counseling sessions per issue per year. The EAP line is available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0142.

## Prescription Drugs

| Drug tier | Helios Standard | Helios Plus |
| Generic | $10 | $5 |
| Preferred brand | $40 | $25 |
| Specialty | 20% up to $250 | 10% up to $150 |

Mail-order pharmacy provides a 90-day supply for two times the retail copay.

## Wellness Benefits

Every employee receives a $600 annual wellness stipend that can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, or meditation apps. Submit receipts in Expensify under the category Wellness. Completing the annual biometric screening earns an additional $300 contribution to your HSA or a $300 payroll credit if you are enrolled in Helios Plus.

## How to Enroll or Make Changes

New hires must enroll in Workday under Benefits within 30 days of their start date. If you do not enroll, you are automatically enrolled in Helios Standard with employee-only coverage. Qualifying life events such as marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage allow plan changes within 31 days of the event. Questions can be sent to benefits@heliosdynamics.example or posted in the #ask-benefits Slack channel.
